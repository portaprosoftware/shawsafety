Note: the published terms are a draft pending legal review and are marked on
the site as not yet constituting a binding agreement.

**Orders and acceptance.** Placing an order is an offer to purchase. It is
accepted when Shaw Safety confirms by email and dispatches the goods. Any order
may be declined or cancelled, including where a product is mispriced or out of
stock, with a refund of anything already charged.

**Pricing.** Prices are in U.S. dollars and exclude applicable taxes and, where
noted, freight. Volume tiers apply automatically based on order quantity.
Published prices may change at any time, but the price confirmed at checkout
governs that order.

**Shipping.** Orders placed before 5pm ET on a business day are dispatched the
next business day, subject to stock. Delivery estimates are estimates, not
guarantees. Risk of loss passes on delivery to the carrier.

**Returns.** Unopened packs may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a
full refund. Return shipping is the buyer's responsibility except where goods
are defective or shipped in error.

**Specifications and compliance.** Published specifications, listings, and
ratings come from the manufacturers and are believed accurate. It remains the
buyer's responsibility to confirm a product meets the requirements of their
operation, terminal, or jurisdiction before use.

**Limitation of liability.** To the maximum extent permitted by law, liability
arising from any order is limited to the amount paid for the goods giving rise
to the claim. Shaw Safety is not liable for indirect, incidental, or
consequential loss.
