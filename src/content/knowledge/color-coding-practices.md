All four tie colors are physically identical — the same 11-inch, 75-lb tensile,
UL 21S listed tie — so the choice of color is an operational one rather than a
technical one.

Fleets typically assign a color per lane, per shift, or per month. Rotating the
color on a schedule makes a broken or swapped seal obvious at a glance: a tie
that does not match the current period's color is immediately suspect without
anyone having to read a number.

Because quantity totals across colors, running a multi-color rotation costs
nothing in volume discount — 50 packs of yellow plus 50 of pink reaches the
100-pack tier exactly as 100 packs of one color would. Fluorescent yellow is
the highest-volume color and is priced as the lead-in SKU, so a rotation
anchored on yellow is marginally cheaper than one that is not.
