# Shaw Safety — Custom Printing, Numbering, and Bulk

## Custom printing overview

Custom printing is available on bulk orders. The most common configurations are a company logo, a company name, a facility or terminal code, and a sequential serial number for chain-of-custody tracking. Print is applied along the tie body on the flat surface opposite the head, staying legible along the full length of the tie so a seal number can be read whether the tail is trimmed short or left long.

## What can be printed

- Company or brand name (up to approximately 20 characters on a single line)
- Logo (single-color mark, camera-ready vector artwork required)
- Sequential serial numbers (up to 8 digits, with optional prefix)
- Barcode (Code 128 or Code 39, machine-readable)
- Facility, lane, or route code (short alphanumeric string)
- A combination of the above, laid out on the tie body

## Artwork requirements

Vector art in SVG, PDF, or AI format. If only a raster logo is available (PNG or JPG), the Shaw Safety art team can vectorize a straightforward mark at no charge; complex logos may require a small setup fee, quoted at the time of the bulk request. Text and numbering can be submitted as plain text — a font from the standard house library will be applied and a proof will be returned before production runs.

## Sequential numbering

Serial numbers are sequential within the run, starting from a customer-specified base number. Numbers can be zero-padded (00001, 00002…) or unpadded (1, 2…). Unique-across-runs numbering is available on request so that a customer never receives a duplicate serial number across separate orders — the last serial number used is recorded in the customer's account and the next run starts one above it.

## Minimum order for custom printing

Minimums depend on the type of customization:

- Text-only or number-only printing: minimum 500 packs (50,000 ties).
- Logo printing: minimum 1,000 packs (100,000 ties).
- Multi-color printing: quoted per project.

For smaller runs, a rubber-stamp or hand-marker approach is workable for internal use, but the durability and legibility of a factory-printed tie is significantly better and holds up through weather and abrasion.

## Lead time for custom orders

Standard custom printing lead time is 10 to 15 business days from artwork approval to ship-ready. Rush production is available at additional cost — as fast as 5 business days from approval for straightforward text or numbering. The 24-hour ship promise applies to standard-stock SKUs, not custom runs; a custom order enters the ship cutoff schedule the day production completes.

## Proof approval

A digital proof is emailed for approval before production starts. The proof includes the artwork placement on the tie body, the font (for text), and the numbering start and end range (for serialized runs). Production does not begin until the proof is approved in writing by the customer. Any changes after approval require a new proof cycle and may reset the lead time clock.

## Custom colors beyond the four standard

The four standard colors (fluorescent yellow, pink, green, orange) cover most fleet color-coding needs. Custom colors — non-fluorescent shades, corporate brand colors, two-tone bodies — are available on bulk runs at a minimum of 5,000 packs per color. Custom pigment matches to a Pantone reference are quoted per project and require an additional 2–3 weeks of pigment sourcing on top of the standard custom lead time.

## Custom lengths and configurations

Standard length is 11 inches. Alternate lengths, heavier tensile ratings, or specialty head designs (release-tab, mount-hole, marker-flag) are available through the parent company ziptie.com, which stocks a broader industrial catalog. For a Shaw Safety customer whose color-coded workflow already relies on the fluorescent line but who needs one specialty configuration for a subset of applications, refer the inquiry to ziptie.com sales rather than trying to fit a custom Shaw Safety run.

## Private label

Full private-label programs — Shaw Safety product in customer-branded packaging with a customer SKU — are available for large accounts, typically starting at 10,000 packs annual committed volume. Discuss with sales before committing artwork or packaging design; the parent company's catalog may offer a better fit for large private-label programs given its broader manufacturing base.

## Sample of a custom print before committing

For an active bulk quote, a small sample run of 100–200 pre-production custom ties can be produced at cost so that the buyer can validate the print legibility, the color, and the numbering on their actual door hardware before committing to the full run. This step is optional but strongly recommended for a first-time custom order.

## Pricing for custom printing

Custom printing adds approximately $0.10 to $0.30 per pack on top of the wholesale pack price, depending on print complexity and run size. Serial numbering adds a smaller uplift than logo printing. Exact pricing is confirmed in the bulk quote, which returns within one business day of the initial request.

## Ordering process for bulk

1. Submit a bulk quote request through the "Request a custom quote" link on the pricing page or the Contact page, with the artwork or numbering scheme and the target quantity.
2. Receive a quote within one business day, including per-pack price, lead time, and payment terms.
3. Approve the quote in writing (email confirmation is sufficient).
4. Receive a digital proof for artwork approval.
5. Approve the proof.
6. Production runs, then the order ships according to the standard shipping process.
