Ordering is self-serve. Pick a product, choose a color, set a quantity in packs,
and add it to the cart. The volume tier and the running subtotal update as the
quantity changes. No account is required.

Checkout hands off to a hosted payment page run by the payment processor
(Stripe), so card details are entered there and never touch Shaw Safety's own
servers. Once payment completes, the shopper lands on an order-confirmed page,
the cart is emptied, and a receipt is emailed. The order then ships the next
business day.

Placing an order is an offer to purchase; it is accepted when Shaw Safety
confirms by email and dispatches the goods. An order may be declined or
cancelled, for example where a product is mispriced or out of stock, and any
amount already charged is refunded.
