## Retail vs wholesale — the whole model

Shaw Safety publishes one price list and applies the discount automatically at
checkout based on total cart quantity. There is no login gate, no negotiation,
no sales call, and no minimum order. Retail buyers pay the published retail
price. Wholesale buyers reach the wholesale price the moment their cart totals
100 packs or more. This is the entire model — it is what "sold direct at
wholesale pricing" refers to.

## Volume tier structure

Volume tiers are total-cart quantities across all four colors combined.

- **1–99 packs** — retail pricing.
- **100–199 packs** — the middle tier, roughly 11% off retail.
- **200+ packs** — the deepest tier, up to 19% off retail.

The discount appears on every product page as the "from" price under the retail
price, and it recalculates in the cart as items are added. There is no code to
enter and no account to create.

## Price by SKU

Prices are per 100-pack.

**Fluorescent yellow** (the lead-in SKU, cheapest color):

- 1–99 packs: $2.69 per pack
- 100–199 packs: $2.39 per pack
- 200+ packs: $2.19 per pack

**Fluorescent pink, green, and orange:**

- 1–99 packs: $2.99 per pack
- 100–199 packs: $2.69 per pack
- 200+ packs: $2.49 per pack

Yellow is priced as the lead-in SKU. The other three colors carry a small color
premium reflecting pigment lot cost, not a difference in construction, tensile,
or compliance.

## Cost per tie

Each pack contains 100 ties. At the deepest yellow tier the cost works out to
about 2.2 cents per tie; at retail across the four colors it averages closer to
2.9 cents. For a fleet running four seals per truck per day across a 50-truck
operation, that puts annualized seal spend under $2,000 at wholesale — a small
line item compared to the loss exposure a broken seal represents.

## Mixed colors count toward the volume tier

Total pack count across every color in the cart determines the tier. Ordering
50 yellow and 50 pink reaches the 100-pack tier exactly the same as ordering
100 yellow. Ordering 60 yellow, 40 pink, 60 green, and 40 orange reaches the
200-pack tier. There is no requirement to hit the tier within a single color.

## No account required

Retail and standard wholesale orders can be placed as a guest checkout. Email,
shipping address, and payment are the only fields required. Creating an account
speeds up reordering, stores billing and shipping addresses, and gives access
to order history and past invoices, but it is optional.

## Custom quote for large orders

For orders that stretch beyond the published 200+ tier — for example, a
full-year committed volume for a large fleet — request a custom quote through
the bulk quote form. The quote comes back with a firm per-pack price and
payment terms.

## Bulk quote workflow

Bulk quote requests should include:

- Approximate annual volume in packs (or tie count).
- Color breakdown, or "same tier applies to any mix."
- Ship-to location(s) and preferred delivery cadence (single shipment, monthly
  release, blanket order).
- Any documentation the receiving terminal requires (UL letter, SDS, statement
  of origin).

Turnaround on a bulk quote is one business day for standard configurations.

## Net terms

Net terms are available on request for wholesale customers with an established
purchasing history or a completed credit application. Standard terms once
approved are Net 30. Larger accounts and government buyers can request Net 45
or Net 60 with prior approval. For a first order, prepayment by card, ACH, or
wire is the fastest path; net terms can be opened for subsequent orders once
the first invoice clears.

## Purchase orders

Purchase orders are accepted for wholesale orders. Send the PO to the address
on the contact page along with the ship-to and bill-to addresses. If the PO
references a specific line item spec, include it so that a written confirmation
of match can be returned before the order enters production.

## Payment methods

- Credit card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover) — all order sizes.
- ACH — wholesale orders on request.
- Wire transfer — wholesale orders on request, useful for larger prepayments.
- Net terms — see the net terms section above.

## Sales tax

Sales tax is collected in states where Shaw Safety has an established nexus.
For tax-exempt buyers (resellers, government, certain non-profits), upload the
exemption certificate through the contact page before ordering and the tax line
will be removed from the cart. Exemption certificates on file are applied to
all subsequent orders under the same account.

## Order cancellation and modification

Standard-stock orders can be modified or canceled at no charge up until the
shipping cutoff on the day the order was placed. After the cutoff, the order is
in the pick queue and can typically still be pulled if a request is submitted
before 8am Eastern the next business day. Custom or printed orders can be
modified until artwork is approved and cannot be canceled once production has
started.
