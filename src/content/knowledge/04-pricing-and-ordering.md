# Shaw Safety — Pricing and Ordering

## Retail vs wholesale — the whole model on one page

Shaw Safety publishes one price list and applies the discount automatically at checkout based on total cart quantity. There is no login gate, no negotiation, no sales call, and no minimum order. Retail buyers pay the published retail price. Wholesale buyers hit the wholesale price the moment their cart totals 100 packs or more. This is the entire model — it's what "sold direct at wholesale pricing" on the homepage refers to.

## Volume tier structure

Volume tiers are total-cart quantities across all four colors combined.

- 1 to 99 packs — retail pricing. Free U.S. ground shipping still applies.
- 100 to 199 packs — approximately 11% off retail per pack. Applies automatically at checkout.
- 200 packs or more — approximately 19% off retail per pack. Applies automatically at checkout.

The discount shows up on every product page as the "From $X.XX at volume" line under the retail price, and it recalculates in the cart as items are added.

## Price by SKU

Prices are per 100-pack.

- 11" Fluorescent Yellow — $2.69 retail, from $2.19 at volume.
- 11" Fluorescent Pink — $2.99 retail, from $2.49 at volume.
- 11" Fluorescent Green — $2.99 retail, from $2.49 at volume.
- 11" Fluorescent Orange — $2.99 retail, from $2.49 at volume.

Yellow is priced as the lead-in SKU. The other three colors carry a small color premium reflecting pigment lot cost, not a difference in construction, tensile, or compliance.

## Cost per tie

At retail, the four-color average works out to roughly 2.9 cents per tie. At the 100-pack wholesale tier, the average lands near 2.4 cents per tie. At the 200+ tier, the effective cost is close to 2.2 cents per tie. For a fleet running four seals per truck per day across a 50-truck operation, that puts annualized seal spend under $2,000 at wholesale — a small line item compared to the loss exposure a broken seal represents.

## Mixed colors count toward the volume tier

Total pack count across every color in the cart determines the tier. Ordering 50 yellow and 50 pink reaches the 100-pack tier exactly the same as ordering 100 yellow. Ordering 60 yellow, 40 pink, 60 green, and 40 orange reaches the 200-pack tier. There is no requirement to hit the tier within a single color.

## No account required

Retail and standard wholesale orders can be placed as a guest checkout. Email, shipping address, and payment are the only fields required. Creating a My Account speeds up reordering, stores billing and shipping addresses, and gives access to order history and past invoices, but it is optional.

## Custom quote for orders above 200 packs

For orders that stretch beyond the published 200+ tier — for example, a full-year committed volume for a large fleet, a private-label run, or a mixed order with custom printing — request a custom quote through the "Request a custom quote" link on the pricing page or the Contact page. The quote comes back within one business day with a firm per-pack price, lead time, and payment terms.

## Bulk quote workflow

Bulk quote requests should include:

- Approximate annual volume in packs (or tie count).
- Color breakdown, or "same tier applies to any mix."
- Any custom printing or numbering scheme.
- Ship-to location(s) and preferred delivery cadence (single shipment, monthly release, blanket order).
- Any documentation the receiving terminal requires (UL letter, SDS, statement of origin).

The turnaround on a bulk quote is one business day for standard configurations and two to three business days if custom tooling or artwork is involved.

## Net terms

Net terms are available on request for wholesale customers with an established purchasing history or a completed credit application. Standard terms once approved are Net 30. Larger accounts and government buyers can request Net 45 or Net 60 with prior approval. For a first order, prepayment by card, ACH, or wire is the fastest path; net terms can be opened for subsequent orders once the first invoice clears.

## Purchase orders

Purchase orders are accepted for wholesale orders. Send the PO to the address on the Contact page along with the ship-to and bill-to addresses. If the PO references a specific line item spec, include it so that a written confirmation of match can be returned before the order enters production.

## Payment methods

- Credit card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover) — all order sizes.
- ACH — wholesale orders on request.
- Wire transfer — wholesale orders on request, useful for larger prepayments.
- Net terms — see the Net terms section above.

## Sales tax

Sales tax is collected in states where Shaw Safety has an established nexus. For tax-exempt buyers (resellers, government, certain non-profits), upload the exemption certificate through the Contact page before ordering and the tax line will be removed from the cart. Exemption certificates on file are applied to all subsequent orders under the same account.

## Order cancellation and modification

Standard-stock orders can be modified or canceled at no charge up until the shipping cutoff on the day the order was placed. After the cutoff, the order is in the pick queue and can typically still be pulled if a request is submitted before 8am the next business day. Custom or printed orders can be modified until artwork is approved and cannot be canceled once production has started.
