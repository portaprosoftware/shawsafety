Free ground shipping is offered on retail orders within the contiguous United
States. The site banner reads "Free U.S. Shipping On Retail Orders". Freight
quotes are provided separately on pallet-scale wholesale orders; ask for one
with a bulk quote request.

Prices are shown in U.S. dollars and exclude applicable taxes and, where noted,
freight. Risk of loss passes on delivery to the carrier.

Ground transit maps published on the site cover the contiguous United States
plus Alaska and Hawaii, both of which sit in the five-day transit band.
