Yes — mixed colors count toward the same volume tier. Quantity is totaled
across every color and SKU in the cart, so ordering 50 packs of fluorescent
yellow and 50 packs of fluorescent pink reaches the 100-pack tier just as 100
packs of yellow would. A mixed pallet reaches the same tier a single-color one
would.

The same rule applies to the vests: a combined lime-and-orange order totals
toward the vest tiers.

Note that each color still bills at its own ladder — fluorescent yellow is the
lead-in SKU and is priced a step below the other three colors — but the
quantity that decides which rung of the ladder applies is the combined one.
