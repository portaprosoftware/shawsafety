Shaw Safety sells two things to people who reorder them every month, and that
focus is the whole business model.

Securing a container is not a complicated purchase. A yard needs a tie that
meets the spec, that an inspector can read from a distance, and that arrives
before the next load does. Most of the cost in this category comes from
everything wrapped around that: quote cycles, account minimums, distributor
margin, and lead times measured in weeks.

Shaw Safety strips that out. It stocks a short catalogue, publishes the price
ladder in full, and ships from inventory the next business day. The tie is the
same one a buyer is purchasing today; the paperwork around it is not. The
promise on the site is "same compliance, same visibility, lower price."
