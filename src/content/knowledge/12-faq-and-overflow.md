# Shaw Safety — FAQ Mirror and Overflow Questions

## Are these ties approved for intermodal container security?

Yes. The 11" fluorescent tie is UL 21S listed and DOT compliant, and its length and tensile rating are sized for standard intermodal container locking mechanisms. If your terminal has a written seal specification, send it over and Shaw Safety will confirm the match in writing before you order. For international ocean container shipping under a C-TPAT program, a plastic tie is not the correct product regardless of tensile — CBP requires an ISO 17712 Class H bolt seal or heavy cable seal, available from the parent company ziptie.com.

## How does volume pricing work?

The discount is published on every product page and applies automatically at checkout. Order 1–99 packs and you pay retail; 100–199 packs drops the price roughly 11%; 200 packs or more drops it roughly 19%. There is no account to create, no minimum order, and no sales call in between. Mixed colors count toward the same volume tier.

## Do mixed colors count toward the same volume tier?

Yes. Total cart quantity across every color determines the tier, so 50 yellow and 50 pink reaches the 100-pack tier the same as 100 yellow. This is how fleets keep four-color coverage on the shelf without giving up wholesale pricing.

## When will my order ship?

## What is the difference between the four colors?

Nothing but the pigment. All four are the same 11-inch, 75-lb tensile, UL 21S listed tie. Fleets typically assign a color per lane, per shift, or per month so a broken or swapped seal is obvious at a glance. Fluorescent yellow is the highest-volume color and is priced as the lead-in SKU.

## Are these C-TPAT compliant?

No, and this is important. C-TPAT requires ISO 17712 Class H seals — steel bolt seals or heavy cable seals with third-party lab certification — on loaded ocean containers bound for the United States. A plastic zip tie is at most an ISO 17712 Class I (indicative) seal and cannot substitute for a Class H seal in a C-TPAT lane. If a C-TPAT-compliant seal is what your operation requires, ziptie.com carries the full C-TPAT product line.

## Are these tamper-evident?

Yes. Single-use self-locking pawl construction. Once the tail is drawn through the head, the pawl engages and cannot be released without cutting the tie. Any attempt to defeat the seal will show either a deformed head, a cut-and-re-tied body, or a color mismatch against the expected lane or shift color.

## Can I reuse the ties?

No. Single-use is what makes the tie tamper-evident. Once seated, the pawl cannot be released without damaging the head. Cut ties should be disposed of, not re-applied.

## What tools do I need to remove them?

A pair of side-cutters, diagonal pliers, or a seal-cutter tool. Every driver in the fleet should carry one. Do not attempt to pry the head — the head is designed to not release, and prying deforms the head and leaves inconclusive tamper evidence.

## Do these work in cold weather?

Yes. The working temperature range extends to -40 °F. The material becomes stiffer at low temperature, so seat the pawl firmly and give the tail a solid tug to confirm engagement — that's the only operational adjustment needed for winter use.

## Do these work in hot weather?

Yes. The working temperature range extends to +185 °F, with short-term excursions permitted to +230 °F. Trailer skin temperatures under southern summer sun stay well within the rating.

## Are these UV resistant for long-term outdoor use?

Not designed for long-term continuous outdoor exposure. The tie is fluorescent-pigmented nylon 66 and is intended for single-use sealing where exposure is measured in hours to days, not months. For a permanent outdoor cable-management application, use a UV-stabilized black tie from ziptie.com.

## What's the shelf life on unopened packs?

Approximately 5 years from the manufacturing date printed on the lot code, stored in a cool, dry, indoor environment out of direct sunlight.

Country of origin is disclosed on request; ask through the Contact page and a statement of origin is returned for the specific lot. For customers whose procurement policy requires U.S.-manufactured product, contact sales for availability of a domestic production run.

## What size trailer or container do these fit?

Any standard intermodal container (20', 40', 45', 53' domestic), any standard dry van or refrigerated trailer with a keeper-and-cam door pattern, and most rear-swing-door hardware on straight trucks and box trucks. Maximum bundle diameter is 3 inches; anything beyond that requires a longer tie from ziptie.com.

## Can I use these on drums, totes, or roll-off boxes?

Yes. Roll-off box door latches, IBC tote lids, and 55-gallon drum ring closures all fit within the 3-inch bundle diameter. Fluorescent color makes an intact seal obvious at a distance across a yard or waste-transfer facility.

## Are these food-safe?

The base polyamide 6.6 is not FDA food-contact certified through Shaw Safety's default supply chain. For a food-contact application, contact sales — a food-grade material variant can be sourced through the parent company on bulk orders. The tie itself, applied externally to a food-truck or refrigerated-trailer door, is fine; the concern is only for direct food contact.
