## Are these ties approved for intermodal container security?

Yes. The 11" fluorescent tie is UL 21S listed and DOT compliant, and its length
and tensile rating are sized for standard intermodal container locking
mechanisms. If your terminal has a written seal specification, send it over and
Shaw Safety will confirm the match in writing before you order.

For international ocean container shipping under a C-TPAT program, a plastic tie
is not the correct product regardless of tensile — CBP requires an ISO 17712
Class H bolt seal or heavy cable seal, which Shaw Safety does not sell.

## How does volume pricing work?

The discount is published on every product page and applies automatically at
checkout. Order 1–99 packs and you pay retail; 100–199 packs is the middle tier
at roughly 11% off; 200 packs or more reaches the deepest tier at up to 19% off.
There is no account to create, no minimum order, and no sales call in between.
Mixed colors count toward the same volume tier.

## Do mixed colors count toward the same volume tier?

Yes. Total cart quantity across every color determines the tier, so 50 yellow
and 50 pink reaches the 100-pack tier the same as 100 yellow. This is how fleets
keep four-color coverage on the shelf without giving up wholesale pricing.

## When will my order ship?

Orders placed before 5pm Eastern on a business day ship the next business day
from stock. Dispatch is subject to stock availability.

## What is the difference between the four colors?

Nothing but the pigment. All four are the same 11-inch, 75-lb tensile, UL 21S
listed tie. Fleets typically assign a color per lane, per shift, or per month so
a broken or swapped seal is obvious at a glance. Fluorescent yellow is the
highest-volume color and is priced as the lead-in SKU, so it sits one step below
the other three at every quantity.

## Can I get our logo or a serial number printed on the ties?

Yes, on bulk orders. Send a bulk quote request with your artwork or numbering
scheme and sales will confirm pricing, minimums, and lead time for your specific
configuration.

## What is your return policy?

Unopened packs can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. If
a tie fails to meet its published spec, tell Shaw Safety and the affected lot is
replaced at no cost. Custom-printed and privately-labeled orders are
non-returnable except in the case of a manufacturing defect or an error against
the approved proof.

## Are these C-TPAT compliant?

No, and this is important. C-TPAT requires ISO 17712 Class H seals — steel bolt
seals or heavy cable seals with third-party lab certification — on loaded ocean
containers bound for the United States. A plastic zip tie is at most an ISO
17712 Class I (indicative) seal and cannot substitute for a Class H seal in a
C-TPAT lane.

Shaw Safety does not sell C-TPAT-qualifying seals. If that is what your
operation requires, you need a high-security barrier seal from a supplier of
that product class.

## Are these tamper-evident?

Yes. Single-use self-locking pawl construction. Once the tail is drawn through
the head, the pawl engages and cannot be released without cutting the tie. Any
attempt to defeat the seal will show either a deformed head, a cut-and-re-tied
body, or a color mismatch against the expected lane or shift color.

## Can I reuse the ties?

No. Single-use is what makes the tie tamper-evident. Once seated, the pawl
cannot be released without damaging the head. Cut ties should be disposed of,
not re-applied.

## What tools do I need to remove them?

A pair of side-cutters, diagonal pliers, or a seal-cutter tool. Every driver in
the fleet should carry one. Do not attempt to pry the head — the head is
designed to not release, and prying deforms the head and leaves inconclusive
tamper evidence.

## Do these work in cold weather?

Yes. The working temperature range extends to -40 °F. The material becomes
stiffer at low temperature, so seat the pawl firmly and give the tail a solid
tug to confirm engagement — that is the only operational adjustment needed for
winter use.

## Do these work in hot weather?

Yes. The working temperature range extends to +185 °F, with short-term
excursions permitted to +230 °F. Trailer skin temperatures under southern summer
sun stay well within the rating.

## Do you ship internationally?

Not directly. Shaw Safety ships within the U.S. and Puerto Rico from the
storefront. For Canadian or Mexican delivery, contact sales — customer-arranged
broker pickup can be accommodated.

## Do you offer samples?

For an active bulk quote, a small sample can be provided so a buyer can validate
the fit on their door hardware before committing to a full run. Small individual
retail samples are not offered because a single 100-pack starts at under $3 and
is faster to just order.

## What is the minimum order?

No minimum. A single 100-pack at $2.69 with free U.S. ground shipping on retail
orders is a valid order.

## Do you accept purchase orders?

Yes, for wholesale orders. Send the PO to the address on the contact page. If
the PO references a specific line item spec, include it so a written confirmation
of match can be returned before the order enters the pick queue.

## Do you offer net terms?

Yes, on approved credit. Standard is Net 30. Larger accounts and government
buyers can request Net 45 or Net 60. First-time orders typically go on card,
ACH, or wire; net terms open for subsequent orders once the first invoice
clears.

## Do you charge sales tax?

Sales tax is collected in states where Shaw Safety has established nexus.
Tax-exempt buyers should upload a current exemption certificate through the
contact page before ordering; the tax line is then removed for all subsequent
orders under the same account.

## Do you offer a discount for government or non-profit buyers?

Standard published volume pricing already sits at direct-wholesale levels. For
large committed volumes — a full-fiscal-year contract for a municipal fleet or a
national non-profit — contact sales for a custom quote. Tax-exempt status is
honored on presentation of a current certificate.

## Can I get a spec sheet before ordering?

Yes. Every product page has a spec link that opens the full engineering drawing
including tolerances, tensile rating, material declaration, and compliance
marks. No account required to view or download.

## Can I get a UL listing letter or SDS?

Yes, at no charge, through the contact page. Standard documentation returns
within one business day. Includes the UL 21S listing letter, SDS, RoHS and REACH
declarations, halogen-free declaration, statement of origin, and batch/lot
traceability.

## What is the shelf life on unopened packs?

Approximately 5 years from the manufacturing date printed on the lot code,
stored in a cool, dry, indoor environment out of direct sunlight.

## Are these made in the USA?

Country of origin is disclosed on request; ask through the contact page and a
statement of origin is returned for the specific lot. For customers whose
procurement policy requires U.S.-manufactured product, contact sales for
availability of a domestic production run.

## What size trailer or container do these fit?

Any standard intermodal container (20', 40', 45', 53' domestic), any standard
dry van or refrigerated trailer with a keeper-and-cam door pattern, and most
rear-swing-door hardware on straight trucks and box trucks. Maximum bundle
diameter is 3 inches; anything beyond that needs a longer tie than the 11-inch
SKU.

## Can I use these on drums, totes, or roll-off boxes?

Yes. Roll-off box door latches, IBC tote lids, and 55-gallon drum ring closures
all fit within the 3-inch bundle diameter. Fluorescent color makes an intact
seal obvious at a distance across a yard or waste-transfer facility.

## Are these food-safe?

The base polyamide 6.6 is not FDA food-contact certified. For a food-contact
application, contact sales before ordering. The tie itself, applied externally
to a food-truck or refrigerated-trailer door, is fine; the concern is only for
direct food contact.

## What is the difference between this and what my current supplier charges?

Direct-wholesale pricing is roughly 30–50% below the going rate at national
industrial safety distributors for a Type 21S 11-inch tie in fluorescent colors.
Fleets switching from a distributor to Shaw Safety typically see their per-tie
cost drop from $0.05–$0.08 down to $0.02–$0.03. The catalog is narrower on
purpose; the savings come from cutting the distributor margin, not from cutting
the product spec.

Yes. Business hours are Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm Eastern. The contact
form routes directly to a team member, and larger accounts can request a call
from the founder or head of sales for anything that needs a longer conversation
than a bulk quote form allows.
