## Product line overview

Shaw Safety sells a focused catalog built for logistics operators who need to
lock a trailer, prove the seal to a driver or inspector, and move on. There are
two product families: fluorescent safety ties (four SKUs, one length, four
colors) and ANSI/ISEA 107 Class 2 hi-vis vests. Everything ships from U.S.
stock and is intended for domestic trucking, intermodal drayage, LTL, and yard
operations. Shaw Safety sells direct at wholesale pricing. No account is
required to order.

## The four fluorescent zip tie SKUs

All four ties are identical in construction, dimensions, tensile strength, and
compliance — the only difference is the pigment. Fleets typically pick a color
per lane, per shift, per month, or per driver so a broken or swapped seal is
immediately obvious at a glance without needing to read a serial number.

- 11" Fluorescent Yellow Zip Tie, 100-Pack — the lead-in SKU and highest-volume
  color. Retail $2.69 per pack, down to $2.19 at volume.
- 11" Fluorescent Pink Zip Tie, 100-Pack — retail $2.99 per pack, down to $2.49
  at volume.
- 11" Fluorescent Green Zip Tie, 100-Pack — retail $2.99 per pack, down to
  $2.49 at volume.
- 11" Fluorescent Orange Zip Tie, 100-Pack — retail $2.99 per pack, down to
  $2.49 at volume.

## Full mechanical specification

The specification is shared across all four color SKUs and is printed on the
spec sheet available from every product page.

- Overall length: 280.0 ± 3 mm (approximately 11 inches)
- Head dimensions: 5.80 ± 0.3 mm × 8.80 ± 0.3 mm × 4.40 ± 0.2 mm
- Body thickness: 1.35 ± 0.15 mm
- Tail taper length: 17.00 ± 1 mm
- Tolerance class: 3.2 unspecified (mm / inch)
- Angular tolerance: ± 2°

The published body width is 0.19 inches (4.8 mm); see the product page
specification list.

## Bundle diameter and loop geometry

The tie is sized for the locking mechanisms found on standard intermodal
container door hardware, roll-up trailer latches, air-cargo ULD locks, and rear
swing-door hasps.

- Maximum bundle diameter: 76.2 mm (approximately 3 inches)
- Minimum bundle diameter: 3.0 mm
- Loop closure: self-locking pawl, single-use once seated
- Head style: standard low-profile square head with internal pawl

## Tensile strength

Rated tensile strength is 334 N, which equals 75 lb of pull force before
failure. This exceeds the UL 62275 Type 21S minimum requirement of 50 lb
(220 N). In practice, a 75-lb rating is well past what a driver or inspector
will apply during a routine seal check, and is sized to hold through road
vibration, yard handling, and weather without creeping.

## Material composition

The tie body and head are molded from a single material system with a color
additive.

- Base polymer: Polyamide 6.6 (Nylon 66), natural resin
- Colorant: fluorescent pigment specific to each SKU (yellow, pink, green, or
  orange)
- No metal components, no lead, no cadmium, no halogens
- Halogen-free construction, suitable for plenum installation (AH-2 rated)

## Flammability rating

- UL 94 V-2 flame classification for the base material
- Plenum-rated AH-2 under UL 62275 (smoke and heat release compliant for cable
  ties installed in air-handling spaces)

## Temperature ratings

The tie is engineered for outdoor trucking, unheated yard use, and refrigerated
trailer applications.

- Working temperature range: -40 °C to +85 °C (-40 °F to +185 °F)
- Installation temperature range: 0 °C to +85 °C (recommended)
- Maximum short-term sustained temperature: +110 °C
- Cold-weather note: Nylon 66 remains functional at -40 °F but becomes stiffer.
  In extreme cold, seat the pawl fully and give the tail a firm tug to confirm
  engagement before releasing.

## Humidity and chemical resistance

- Humidity absorption: 2.4% at 23 °C, 50% relative humidity
- Good resistance to bases, oils, greases, and oil derivatives
- Limited resistance to chloride solvents
- Not resistant to phenols

For fuel-splash environments or chemical tanker applications, the tie is
acceptable for external door sealing but should not be installed where it will
be immersed in solvents.

## Standards conformance summary

- UL 62275 Type 21S (cable management system, higher-tier thermal and material
  qualification)
- UL 94 V-2 (flammability)
- Plenum rated AH-2
- Halogen-free
- Complies with material declarations required for DOT container security ties
  in domestic freight

## What comes in a 100-pack

Each SKU ships as a single 100-count polybag with a printed spec label. The bag
format is intentional: a dispatcher can hand one bag to each driver at shift
start without counting individual ties, and the driver returns any unused ties
in the same bag at end of shift. Bulk pricing applies once total cart quantity
reaches 100 packs, regardless of color mix.

## Spec sheet availability

Every product page has a spec link that opens the full engineering drawing for
that color, including tolerances, tensile rating, material declaration, and
compliance marks. The spec sheets can be downloaded and forwarded to a safety
manager, terminal, or auditor without needing an account. If a specific
customer requires a stamped material declaration or a UL listing letter,
request one through the contact page and it will be provided within one
business day.
