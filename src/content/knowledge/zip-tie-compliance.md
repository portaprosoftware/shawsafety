The 11-inch fluorescent zip tie carries four published compliance marks:

- UL 21S Listed
- DOT Compliant
- Meets Intermodal Container Security Requirements
- RoHS Compliant

Yes, the tie is approved for intermodal container security: it is UL 21S listed
and DOT compliant, and its length and tensile rating are sized for standard
intermodal container locking mechanisms.

If a terminal has a written seal specification, Shaw Safety asks buyers to send
it over and will confirm the match in writing before an order is placed.
Published specifications, listings, and ratings come from the manufacturers and
are believed accurate, but it remains the buyer's responsibility to confirm a
product meets the requirements of their operation, terminal, or jurisdiction
before use.
