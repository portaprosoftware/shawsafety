Shaw Safety states four operating principles on its About page.

**Published pricing.** Every price and every volume tier is on the product
page. Nothing is gated behind an account or a quote, because a dispatcher
reordering seals should not have to schedule a call to learn what they cost.

**One spec that covers the work.** Shaw Safety does not carry twelve
near-identical ties. It carries the 11-inch, 75-lb, UL 21S listed tie that
intermodal work actually calls for, in the four colors fleets use to separate
lanes and shifts.

**Stock, not lead times.** Seals in this category are routinely quoted at four
to six weeks. Shaw Safety's ship the next business day because they are held
in inventory rather than ordered once a customer does.

**Built for intermodal.** Length, tensile rating, and visibility are all chosen
against container locking mechanisms and yard conditions, not adapted from a
general-purpose cable tie.
