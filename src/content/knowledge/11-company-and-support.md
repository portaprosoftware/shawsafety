## What Shaw Safety is

Shaw Safety is a focused direct-wholesale brand selling ANSI/UL-certified
fluorescent safety ties and ANSI/ISEA 107 Class 2 hi-vis vests for logistics
operators. The catalog is intentionally narrow — four fluorescent tie SKUs and a
Class 2 vest — because the target customer (trucking, drayage, intermodal,
waste, portable sanitation, septic) has a specific, repeating purchase need and
does not want to sort through a 500-SKU industrial supply catalog to find it.

Prices are published; there is no account gate, no minimum order, and no sales
call between the product page and the shopping cart.

## Where Shaw Safety operates

Shaw Safety ships from U.S. stock to all 50 states and Puerto Rico, and into
Canada and Mexico via customer-arranged pickup. International direct shipping is
not currently offered. See the ground delivery times map on the landing page for
transit estimates by state.

## Contact channels

- **Contact page:** linked from the top navigation of every page
- **Bulk quotes:** the quote request form on the wholesale page
- **Support:** the same contact form routes to the support team for order
  questions, returns, spec sheets, and documentation requests
- **Chat:** the assistant in the lower-right corner of the site

## Support response times

- Contact form and email inquiries: same business day for messages received
  before 3pm Eastern, next business day for messages after that.
- Bulk quotes: one business day for standard configurations.
- Return and warranty claims: same business day acknowledgment, replacement
  dispatched within one business day of confirming the failure.
- Chat: the assistant answers from published site content at any hour; anything
  it cannot answer routes to the team during business hours.

## Business hours

Standard business hours are Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm Eastern. Orders
placed at any time — including nights, weekends, and holidays — are accepted;
the ship cutoff for next-business-day dispatch is 5pm Eastern on a business day.

## Account features

The optional account section on the site provides:

- Order history and past invoices for reordering and expense reporting.
- Stored shipping and billing addresses.
- Saved payment methods for faster checkout.
- Access to compliance documents (UL letters, SDS, statement of origin) tied to
  past orders.
- For net-terms customers, current account balance and outstanding invoices.

## Reordering

Repeat orders can be placed by adding items to the cart at the product page or
by using the reorder action on any past order in the account section. For fleets
that consume a predictable weekly or monthly volume, contact sales to set up a
standing order — the same order ships on a fixed cadence without needing a new
PO each cycle.

## Payment methods

- All major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover)
- ACH for wholesale
- Wire transfer for wholesale prepayments
- Net terms once approved
- Purchase orders on file for large accounts

## Documentation and audits

Shaw Safety supports the documentation typically requested during a customer
audit or supplier qualification:

- W-9 (U.S. federal tax form)
- Certificate of insurance on request
- Supplier questionnaire completion
- ISO 9001 documentation from the manufacturing source
- SDS, RoHS, REACH, and halogen-free declarations
- UL 21S listing letters by SKU and lot

Request through the contact page; standard documentation is returned within one
business day.

## Privacy and data

Customer information collected at checkout — name, email, shipping address,
billing address, payment method — is used only for fulfilling the order and for
account-related communications. Shaw Safety does not sell customer data to third
parties. Marketing email is opt-in and can be unsubscribed at any time.

## Marketing communications

New customers can opt in to a newsletter with product updates, pricing changes,
and case studies from fleet operators. The newsletter is opt-in, not automatic
on order. Existing customers receive order-related emails (confirmation,
tracking, delivery notification) regardless of marketing opt-in status.

## Physical presence

Shaw Safety operates as a direct-to-fleet distribution model without a public
showroom or walk-in retail. All purchasing happens through the website or
through direct sales contact for bulk quotes. This is deliberate — the model is
built for operators who know what they need and want it shipped, not for
browse-and-compare shoppers.

## Careers and partnerships

Shaw Safety is a lean operation; hiring, dealer inquiries, and reseller
partnerships are handled directly by the founding team. Direct inquiries through
the contact page.

## Complaints and escalation

Any issue that is not resolved through the standard support channel can be
escalated by asking for a supervisor or the founder in a contact form message.
Shaw Safety is small enough that founder-level attention is available for any
customer who needs it, and this is intentional — the direct-wholesale model
depends on a short chain from customer to decision maker.
