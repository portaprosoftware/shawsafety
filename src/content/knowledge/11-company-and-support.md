# Shaw Safety — Company, Contact, and Support

## What Shaw Safety is

Shaw Safety is a focused direct-wholesale brand selling ANSI/UL-certified fluorescent safety ties and ANSI/ISEA 107 Class 2 hi-vis vests for logistics operators. The catalog is intentionally narrow — four fluorescent tie SKUs and a Class 2 vest — because the target customer (trucking, drayage, intermodal, waste, portable sanitation, septic) has a specific, repeating purchase need and does not want to sort through a 500-SKU industrial supply catalog to find it. Prices are published; there is no account gate; no minimum order; and no sales call between the product page and the shopping cart.

## Relationship to ziptie.com

Shaw Safety is a sub-brand under the ziptie.com family. ziptie.com is the parent company and carries a full industrial catalog including ISO 17712 Class H bolt seals, cable seals, pull-tight seals, meter seals, and specialty ties across a wide range of lengths, materials, and tensile ratings. Shaw Safety exists to serve the specific fluorescent-tie-plus-Class-2-vest customer at direct pricing without asking that customer to shop across the broader ziptie.com catalog. Customers whose needs go beyond the four Shaw SKUs — international ocean freight, meter sealing, high-security cable seals, non-fluorescent industrial applications — are referred to ziptie.com. There is no penalty for the referral, and many operators end up buying from both.

## Where Shaw Safety operates

Shaw Safety ships from U.S. stock to all 50 states, Puerto Rico, and (via customer-arranged pickup) into Canada and Mexico. International direct shipping is not currently offered; ziptie.com handles international logistics for the family. See the Ground Delivery Times map on the homepage for transit estimates by state.

## Support response times

- Contact form and email inquiries: same business day for messages received before 3pm Eastern, next business day for messages after that.
- Bulk quotes: one business day for standard configurations, two to three business days for custom tooling or artwork.
- Return and warranty claims: same business day acknowledgment, replacement dispatched within one business day of confirming the failure.
- Chat: staffed during business hours; after-hours messages are answered the next business day.

## Business hours

Standard business hours are Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm Eastern. Orders placed at any time — including nights, weekends, and holidays — are accepted; the ship cutoff for next-business-day dispatch is 5pm Eastern on a business day.

## My Account features

The optional My Account section on the site provides:

- Order history and past invoices for reordering and expense reporting.
- Stored shipping and billing addresses.
- Saved payment methods for faster checkout.
- Access to compliance documents (UL letters, SDS, statement of origin) tied to past orders.
- For net-terms customers, current account balance and outstanding invoices.

## Reordering

Repeat orders can be placed by adding items to the cart at the product page or by using the "Reorder" button on any past order in My Account. For fleets that consume a predictable weekly or monthly volume, contact sales to set up a standing order — the same order ships on a fixed cadence without needing a new PO each cycle.

## Payment methods

- ACH for wholesale
- Wire transfer for wholesale prepayments
- Net terms once approved
- Purchase orders on file for large accounts

## Documentation and audits

Shaw Safety supports the documentation typically requested during a customer audit or supplier qualification:

- W-9 (U.S. federal tax form)
- Certificate of insurance on request
- Supplier questionnaire completion
- References from existing design partners
- ISO 9001 documentation from the manufacturing source
- SDS, RoHS, REACH, halogen-free declarations
- UL 21S listing letters by SKU and lot

Request through the Contact page; standard documentation is returned within one business day.

## Privacy and data

Customer information collected at checkout — name, email, shipping address, billing address, payment method — is used only for fulfilling the order and for account-related communications. Shaw Safety does not sell customer data to third parties. Marketing email is opt-in and can be unsubscribed at any time.

## Marketing communications

New customers can opt in to a monthly newsletter with product updates, pricing changes, and case studies from fleet operators. The newsletter is opt-in, not automatic on order. Existing customers receive order-related emails (confirmation, tracking, delivery notification) regardless of marketing opt-in status.

## Physical presence

Shaw Safety operates as a direct-to-fleet distribution model without a public showroom or walk-in retail. All purchasing happens through the website or through direct sales contact for bulk quotes. This is deliberate — the model is built for operators who know what they need and want it shipped, not for browse-and-compare shoppers.

## Careers and partnerships

Shaw Safety is a lean operation; hiring, dealer inquiries, and reseller partnerships are handled directly by the founding team. Direct inquiries through the Contact page.

## Complaints and escalation

Any issue that isn't resolved through the standard support channel can be escalated by asking for a supervisor or the founder in a Contact form message. Shaw Safety is small enough that founder-level attention is available for any customer who needs it, and this is intentional — the direct-wholesale model depends on a short chain from customer to decision maker.
