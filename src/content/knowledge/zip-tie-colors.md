The 11-inch fluorescent zip tie comes in four colors, each with its own SKU and
all currently in stock:

| Color              | SKU    |
| ------------------ | ------ |
| Fluorescent Yellow | 220178 |
| Fluorescent Pink   | 220179 |
| Fluorescent Green  | 220180 |
| Fluorescent Orange | 220181 |

Nothing differs between them but the pigment. All four are the same 11-inch,
75-lb tensile, UL 21S listed tie. Fleets typically assign a color per lane, per
shift, or per month so a broken seal is obvious at a glance.

Fluorescent yellow is the highest-volume color and is priced as the lead-in
SKU, so it sits one step below the other three at every quantity. The
fluorescent pigment stays legible at a distance and under low dock lighting, so
drivers and inspectors can confirm a seal without breaking stride.
