Note: the published privacy policy is a draft pending legal review.

**What is collected.** Placing an order collects the name, company, email,
phone number, and shipping address provided. Payment card details are handled
entirely by the payment processor and never reach Shaw Safety's servers.

**The cart.** A shopper's cart is stored locally in their own browser so it
survives a reload. It is not transmitted to Shaw Safety until checkout begins,
and clearing browser storage removes it entirely.

**How it is used.** Information is used to process, fulfil, and support orders
and to respond to quote requests. Shaw Safety does not sell customer
information and does not send marketing email unless asked to.

**Sharing.** Only what is necessary is shared with the payment processor and
shipping carriers to complete an order, and with authorities where the law
requires it.

**Retention.** Order records are retained as long as needed for warranty,
accounting, and tax obligations. A copy or deletion of information can be
requested at any time by emailing sales@shawsafety.com.
