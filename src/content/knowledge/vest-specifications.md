Specifications for the Class 2 hi-vis safety vest, as published on the product
page:

- **Standard:** ANSI/ISEA 107-2020 Type R Class 2
- **Material:** 100% polyester mesh, 120 gsm
- **Reflective tape:** 2 inch silver, torso + shoulders
- **Closure:** Hook-and-loop front, 5-point breakaway
- **Pockets:** 2 lower patch, 1 inner radio
- **Sizes:** S through 4XL
- **Pack quantity:** 10 vests

Silver retroreflective banding runs across the torso and over both shoulders
for a full 360 degrees of return under headlights. The 5-point breakaway
releases at both shoulders and both sides plus the front closure if the vest
catches on moving equipment.
