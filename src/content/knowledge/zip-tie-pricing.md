Zip tie pricing is per 100-pack and drops automatically at 100 and 200 packs.

**Fluorescent yellow** (the lead-in SKU, cheapest color):

- 1-99 packs: $2.69 per pack
- 100-199 packs: $2.39 per pack
- 200+ packs: $2.19 per pack

**Fluorescent pink, green, and orange:**

- 1-99 packs: $2.99 per pack
- 100-199 packs: $2.69 per pack
- 200+ packs: $2.49 per pack

Each pack contains 100 ties, so at the deepest yellow tier the cost works out
to about 2.2 cents per tie. Discounts are applied automatically at checkout;
there is no code to enter, no account to create, and no minimum order. The
landing page summarises this as two plans: Retail (1-99 packs, from $2.69 per
100-pack) and Wholesale (100+ packs, from $2.19 per 100-pack, up to 19% off).
