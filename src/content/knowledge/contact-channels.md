Shaw Safety publishes three ways to make contact:

- **Sales & orders:** (800) 555-0117, Monday to Friday, 7am–6pm CT
- **Email:** sales@shawsafety.com, replies within one business day
- **Contact form:** at /contact, with name, company, email, and message

The contact page invites questions about a seal specification, a mixed-color
bulk order, or a printed tie: send the details and Shaw Safety comes back with
pricing and a lead time — not a discovery call.

Bulk quote requests go through the separate form at /wholesale#quote. Privacy
requests and questions about the terms of service also go to
sales@shawsafety.com. Both forms support dictation as well as typing.
