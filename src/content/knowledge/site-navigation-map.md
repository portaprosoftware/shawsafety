The public pages on shawsafety.com:

- `/` — landing page: hero, trust marks, the four tie colors, feature
  highlights, pricing plans, vest cross-sell, FAQ, and the ground delivery map
- `/products` — all products, grouped into Safety Ties and Safety Vests
- `/products/11-inch-zip-tie-100-pack` — the zip tie product page; a color can
  be deep-linked with `?color=fluorescent-pink` and so on
- `/products/class-2-hi-vis-safety-vest` — the vest product page
- `/safety-vests` — vest landing page, including what Class 2 means
- `/wholesale` — published price ladder, volume calculator, and why buy direct
- `/wholesale#quote` — the bulk quote request form
- `/about` — why Shaw Safety exists and how it operates
- `/contact` — phone, email, contact form, and the FAQ
- `/contact#shipping` — shipping and returns
- `/cart` — the cart
- `/terms` and `/privacy` — draft legal pages

Trust marks shown on the landing page: Meets Intermodal Container Security
Requirements, UL 21S Listed, DOT Compliant Security Ties, Direct Wholesale
Pricing, and Ships Next Business Day.
