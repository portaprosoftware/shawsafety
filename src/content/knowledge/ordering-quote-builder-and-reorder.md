## The quote builder

The wholesale page (/wholesale#quote) carries a quote builder. Build the order
line by line, product, color, and quantity in packs, and the stock portion is
priced instantly off the published ladder, with mixed colors counting toward
the combined volume tier. Add-ons that need a person, printed ties, serialized
numbering, mixed vest sizing, pallet freight, net 30 terms, and standing
monthly orders, are ticked on the same form and come back priced within one
business day.

The built quote can be sent to sales as a structured request, printed as a
one-page quote sheet with a reference number for accounts payable, or the
stock lines can be added straight to the cart and bought immediately. When
combined-color pricing is deeper than what per-line online checkout would
charge, the builder says so and recommends sending the quote.

## One-click reorder

Every completed order produces a reorder link. It appears on the order
confirmation page, where it can be copied, and the cart page offers a
"Reorder Your Last Order" button on the same browser. Opening a reorder link
rebuilds the exact basket, quantities included, and current published pricing
applies automatically. The link carries no payment details, only what to put
in the cart.

## The assistant can stage a cart

Telling the site assistant explicit quantities, for example "300 packs of
yellow and 100 of orange", produces a staged cart: a review card with the
priced lines and an Add to cart button. Nothing is purchased by staging; the
customer reviews the cart and checks out through Stripe as normal. The
assistant cannot place, change, or cancel orders, approve terms, or set
discounts; a person handles those.

## The dispatch countdown

The banner at the top of the site counts down to the 5pm ET cutoff on
business days, for example "Order within 2h 14m and it ships tomorrow". After
the cutoff and on weekends it states when an order placed now will ship. The
promise is unchanged: orders placed before 5pm ET on a business day ship the
next business day.
