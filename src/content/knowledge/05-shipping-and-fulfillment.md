# Shaw Safety — Shipping and Fulfillment

## The core commitment: seals ship within 24 hours

Orders placed before 5pm Eastern on a business day ship the next business day from U.S. stock. This is the operating rule that defines the category — most competitors in industrial security seals quote multi-week lead times because they treat every order as a build-to-order run. Shaw Safety stocks the four standard SKUs so a next-day-ship promise is the default, not the exception.

## Free U.S. ground shipping

All retail and wholesale orders shipping within the contiguous 48 states ship free by ground. There is no minimum order size to qualify. Shipping to Alaska, Hawaii, and Puerto Rico is available at published carrier rates calculated at checkout.

## Ground delivery times by region

Business days in transit once the order leaves the dock. All times exclude weekends, holidays, and the ship day itself.

- 1 day: Ohio, Pennsylvania, Indiana, Michigan, Kentucky, West Virginia, Virginia, New York.
- 2 days: Illinois, Wisconsin, Minnesota, Iowa, Missouri, Tennessee, North Carolina, South Carolina, Georgia, Alabama, Vermont, New Hampshire, Maine, Massachusetts, Rhode Island, Connecticut, New Jersey, Maryland, Delaware.
- 3 days: North Dakota, South Dakota, Nebraska, Kansas, Oklahoma, Texas, Louisiana, Arkansas, Mississippi, Florida, Wyoming, Colorado, Utah, Montana.
- 4 days: California, Nevada, Arizona, New Mexico.
- 5 days: Washington, Oregon, Idaho, Alaska, Hawaii.

Transit times are carrier estimates. If a delivery date matters (job start, terminal audit, quarterly rollout), contact sales for a guaranteed delivery confirmation before ordering.

## Expedited shipping

Next-day-air and 2-day-air are available at published carrier rates, quoted at checkout or on request. For custom or printed orders, expedited shipping starts the clock after production is complete, not after the order is placed. For truly time-critical needs — a full trailer rollout scheduled for a specific week — request an expedite through the Contact page and Shaw Safety will confirm carrier options and cost within the same business day.

## Shipping carriers

Standard ground shipments go via a national parcel carrier selected daily based on transit performance and rate to the destination ZIP. For LTL-sized orders (typically 500+ packs to a single address), an LTL carrier is dispatched with a pallet shipment; a phone number for delivery scheduling is required.

## International shipping

Shaw Safety does not currently ship internationally from the storefront. For a Canadian or Mexican delivery, contact sales — customer-arranged pickup by the buyer's own broker or freight forwarder can be accommodated. For international ocean-freight or air-cargo customers who need seals delivered to a foreign facility, the parent company ziptie.com maintains international shipping infrastructure and a full C-TPAT-compliant product line; a referral to ziptie.com is often the correct next step.

## PO Box and residential addresses

PO Box shipping is available for retail-sized orders (typically under 20 packs) via a carrier that services PO boxes. For orders larger than one small parcel, a physical street address is required. Residential deliveries are handled the same as commercial deliveries, though transit times to remote residential ZIPs may add one business day.

## Order tracking

Tracking numbers are emailed to the address used at checkout the moment the label is generated, typically the morning of the ship day. If a tracking email doesn't arrive within one business day of order confirmation, check the spam folder first and then contact support with the order number.

## Delivery signature

Signature is not required by default. For high-value orders or a buyer who wants to require signature on delivery, request the change at checkout in the order notes or contact sales before the ship cutoff.

## Damaged or lost shipments

If a package arrives visibly damaged, note the damage with the carrier at delivery and photograph the outer box and the affected ties. Contact support within 5 business days of delivery with the order number and photos, and Shaw Safety will ship a replacement at no charge and pursue the carrier claim internally. For a lost shipment — no tracking movement for 5+ business days past the estimated delivery date — contact support and a replacement will be dispatched while the original is traced.

## Order confirmation timing

An order confirmation email is sent immediately after checkout. This is the receipt and it should arrive within a few minutes; if it doesn't, the address used may have been mistyped — contact support with the last four digits of the payment card or the shipping ZIP and the order can be located.

## Backorder handling

Standard stock is not typically backordered because the four SKUs are produced against a running forecast. In the rare event a color is temporarily out of stock, the product page will display the estimated back-in-stock date and orders placed against the backorder ship free by expedited service once inventory clears. If timing is critical, choose an in-stock color and use color assignment on the receiving end instead.

## Shipping to multiple addresses

Multi-address shipping is not supported through the standard cart. To ship one order to multiple locations — for example, a fleet with terminals in five states — either place separate orders per address, or contact sales for a bulk quote that includes a shipment schedule and multiple ship-to addresses under a single PO.

## Duties, taxes, and brokerage on cross-border shipments

For customer-arranged pickups destined for Canada or Mexico, duties, taxes, and brokerage are the buyer's responsibility and are not included in the invoice from Shaw Safety.
